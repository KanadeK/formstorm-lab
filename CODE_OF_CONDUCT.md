# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FormStorm Lab a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive, specific feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users.
- Protecting private reports, credentials, personal information, and sensitive test evidence.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Threats, doxxing, discrimination, or encouragement of harm.
- Using review, issue, or security channels to pressure someone into unsafe testing or disclosure.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within repository spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces. Repository spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, release coordination, and private vulnerability reports.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repeated behavior, willingness to repair harm, and the safety of affected participants.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the project owner's GitHub profile. Include links, dates, and a concise description, but do not spread private material more widely than needed. If the concern involves the project owner, use GitHub's platform reporting and blocking tools.

All reports will be reviewed with discretion. The privacy and safety of the reporter and affected people will be considered during handling.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may use the following responses:

1. Correction: a private explanation of the issue and request to change behavior.
2. Warning: a documented warning with conditions for continued participation.
3. Temporary restriction: a time-limited restriction from project interaction.
4. Permanent restriction: removal from future project participation.

Severe threats, harassment, disclosure of private information, or deliberate security harm can move directly to a stronger response.
